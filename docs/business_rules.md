\# Loan Underwriting Business Rules



\## Credit Score Rules



1\. Credit score below 600 is considered high risk.

2\. Credit score between 600 and 699 may require manual underwriting review.

3\. Credit score of 700 or above is considered strong if DTI ratio is acceptable.



\## Debt-to-Income Rules



1\. DTI ratio below 40 percent is generally acceptable.

2\. DTI ratio between 40 and 50 percent may require review.

3\. DTI ratio above 50 percent exceeds standard policy limit.



\## Employment Rules



1\. Full-time employment improves approval eligibility.

2\. Unemployed applicants are not eligible for automatic approval.

3\. Self-employed applicants may require additional income verification.



\## Approval Rules



1\. Approved applications can move to disbursement.

2\. Rejected applications cannot move to disbursement.

3\. Manual review applications require underwriting decision before approval.



\## Lifecycle Stages



1\. Application Intake

2\. Document Verification

3\. Underwriting Review

4\. Approval Decision

5\. Disbursement
