Credit Eligibility Rules

Minimum credit score for approval is 600.

Credit score between 600 and 699 may require manual underwriting review depending on debt-to-income ratio and requested loan amount.

Credit score of 700 or above is considered strong if the applicant also has acceptable income and debt-to-income ratio.

Debt-to-income ratio should generally be 40 percent or lower for low-risk approval.

Debt-to-income ratio above 50 percent exceeds the standard policy limit.

Unemployed applicants are not eligible for automatic approval and require rejection or manual exception handling.

Requested loan amount should be reasonable compared to annual income.
