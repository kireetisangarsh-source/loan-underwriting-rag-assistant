Loan Disbursement Policy

Approved loans can move to disbursement only after identity verification, income verification, document verification, and final underwriting approval are completed.

Applications in manual review cannot be disbursed until the underwriting team completes risk review.

Rejected applications are not eligible for disbursement.

Disbursement should only occur after approval status is marked as Approved.

Processing time should be monitored across application intake, document verification, underwriting review, approval decision, and disbursement stages.
